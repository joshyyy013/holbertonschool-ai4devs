
# Product Idea: TaskFlow

## Vision
TaskFlow is a simple task management application that helps university students organise assignments, track deadlines, and manage their study workload in one place. Its goal is to make planning easier and reduce missed deadlines.

## Users
- **University students**: Need to organise tasks across different courses, see upcoming deadlines, and track progress.
- **Students working on group projects**: Need to assign tasks to team members and monitor shared progress.

## Key Features
- **Task creation and management**: Create, edit, complete, and delete tasks.
- **Deadlines and priorities**: Set due dates and priority levels for each task.
- **Course organisation**: Group tasks by course or project.
- **Progress tracking**: View tasks by status (To Do, In Progress, Done).
- **Deadline reminders**: Receive reminders before upcoming due dates.
- **Group collaboration**: Share project task lists and assign tasks to group members.

## Differentiators
- **Student-focused design**: Organises work around courses, assignments, and group projects rather than general business workflows.
- **Simple interface**: Emphasises quick task entry and a clear overview without complex project management tools.
- **Unified workload view**: Shows tasks from multiple courses and projects together, helping students prioritise their time.
